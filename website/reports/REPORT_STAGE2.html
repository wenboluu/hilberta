How to read these numbers

  • All speed rows come from one harness on single, exclusive H100 80GB GPUs (separate GPUs of one node ran different parts concurrently; a part refuses to start if another process is on its GPU). Competitors run their official code and weights in their own venvs inside the same harness: same prompts, initial latents, warm-up, 3 timed repeats, timing boundary and VRAM accounting; their setup (checkpoint load, processor install, block-mask build) is outside the timed region.
  • Our LoRA is merged into the bf16 weights for timing (deployment form, zero extra cost). Merge gate: same-input rel-L2 merged vs unmerged = 0.005, 0.0053 (< 0.02). Rows with the unmerged FP32 side-branch LoRA are kept as *_lora_unmerged (about 0.8x of Full at 1024) and are not used in the tables. At 2048/4096 no adapter was trained: speed only, quality not claimed (a merged LoRA has the same cost).
  • The 2,048 evaluation images of ours were generated with the Flex64 training backend; the speed numbers use our custom paired inference kernel. Consistency gate with the 4-tile LoRA on 3 teacher-cache states: paired vs Flex64 rel-L2 0.0066, 0.0105, 0.0159 (tolerance 0.02); whole-denoiser paired vs dense-mask SDPA rel-L2 t4k1@1024=0.0095, t16k3@1024=0.0094, t4k1@2048=0.0086, t16k3@2048=0.0082, t4k1@4096=0.0069, t16k3@4096=0.0076.
  • Why 1024 gains are small: at 4,608 tokens attention is about 22% of the denoiser (Full: 0.81 ms/layer x 57 = 46 ms of 208 ms), so even zero-cost attention caps the denoiser speedup near 1.28x. The share grows with tokens (about 50% at 2048, about 78% at 4096), which is where the 1.8x / 4.0x denoiser speedups of 16-tile K3 come from. Kernel latency x 57 layers predicts the measured denoiser time within 1% at 1024 and 2048 (within 7% at 4096).
  • SpargeAttn is sparse and quantized (Q/K INT8, V FP8 inside its kernel); ours and CLEAR run BF16 attention. CLEAR is a full fine-tune of the attention projections (official checkpoint, training steps of the released checkpoint unknown).
  • OOM / unsupported, reported verbatim: CLEAR at 4096 (official create_block_mask requests 195 GiB); every method's denoiser at 4096 batch 8 (MLP activations; batch 4 already peaks at 48 GiB); 16-tile at 512 (build_plan needs >= 256 tokens per tile). CLEAR 2048 denoiser rows were re-measured in a fresh process after an allocator-state OOM; the superseded rows are kept with a note.
  • Competitor configuration screening (section 5) used the competitors' own scripts on 32 development prompts (VAE decode included); the choice rule is lowest latency only. In the unified harness above, both chosen competitors are faster than Full at every point where they ran.
